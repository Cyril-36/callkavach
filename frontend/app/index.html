CallKavach listens only through this device’s microphone. It does not intercept calls, block callers, or decide with certainty that anyone is a criminal. Warning levels and evidence come from the backend; this screen does not calculate a score. While listening, audio goes to this server and on to Sarvam for transcription, and the transcribed text (long numbers masked) goes through the AICredits gateway to Google’s Gemini model for analysis; those providers’ own retention terms apply. This app does not save recordings or transcripts. Prototype: use synthetic or consented audio only.
